## Attribution policy

Material contributions by coding agents receive a `Co-authored-by` trailer
using a verified public identity. Temporary child agents are credited through
their parent. Only participating agents are credited; published history is not
rewritten solely to add attribution. The maintainer retains release authority.

```text
Co-authored-by: Codex <267193182+codex@users.noreply.github.com>
```
